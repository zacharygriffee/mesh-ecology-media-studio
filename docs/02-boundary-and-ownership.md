# Boundary And Ownership

## Studio Owns

Studio owns media-domain meaning:

- why an asset exists
- which card, shot, scene, prompt, reference, or provider job produced it
- what review evidence exists
- whether a local operator accepted or rejected a candidate
- what continuity claims are available for later interpretation

## Studio Does Not Own

Studio does not own:

- Edge's operator-facing control plane
- Spine's system-composition doctrine
- Packs shared control-plane vocabulary
- byte storage infrastructure or materialization proof
- causal-substrate ontology or continuity truth
- mesh runtime behavior, publication authority, or ratification authority

## Practical Rule

Studio performs and records media work. Edge organizes operator attention
around that work. Edge does not become Studio, and Studio does not reinvent
Edge's operator boundary.
