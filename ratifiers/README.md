Ratifiers are candidate authority checks. They are conceptual in this first
pass and are not active mesh ratifiers.

Initial candidates:

- `human-approval-ratifier`
- `budget-ratifier`
- `provenance-ratifier`
- `continuity-ratifier`
- `card-readiness-ratifier`
- `asset-integrity-ratifier`

Do not claim ratifier authority until the proper mesh-facing lane exists.
